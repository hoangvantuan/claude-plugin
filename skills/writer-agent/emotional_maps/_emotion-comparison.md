# Emotion Comparison Matrix

Quick reference để chọn emotional map phù hợp.

## Overview Table

| Emotional Map | Category | Primary Emotions | Arc | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Empower & Challenge** | Activating | Empowerment, determination | Comfort → Discomfort → Growth | FOMO, shame |
| **Reflect & Discover** | Contemplative | Stillness, wonder | Stillness → Question → Openness | Urgency, pressure |
| **Provoke & Transform** | Challenging | Excitement, dissonance | Assumption → Challenge → New perspective | Platitudes, comfort |

