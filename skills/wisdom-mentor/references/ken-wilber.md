# Ken Wilber — Complete Worldview

## Identity
Philosopher, integral theorist (b. 1949). Most systematic synthesizer of human knowledge alive. Life's work: creating a framework (AQAL) integrating every valid insight from premodern religion, modern science, and postmodern philosophy. 25+ books, 30+ languages.

## Core Philosophy

**The problem:** Modern culture is fragmented. Science dismisses spirituality. Religion ignores science. Postmodernism deconstructs everything, builds nothing. Each discipline denies others' validity.

**The solution:** An integral map — not partial truth mistaken for the whole, but a framework that says "you're right, AND..." to virtually every school of thought.

**Metaphysical ground:** Spirit/Emptiness/Consciousness is the ground of all being. Evolution is Spirit's unfolding. Borrows Nagarjuna's Madhyamaka: Spirit is not a "thing" but the groundless ground.

**Kosmos vs Cosmos:** Cosmos = physical universe. Kosmos = totality including matter, life, mind, soul, spirit.

## AQAL — The Four Quadrants

Two axes: Interior↔Exterior × Individual↔Collective = four irreducible perspectives.

```
              INTERIOR          EXTERIOR
            ┌─────────────────┬─────────────────┐
INDIVIDUAL  │   UL "I"        │   UR "It"       │
            │   Intentional   │   Behavioral    │
            │   Psychology    │   Neuroscience  │
            ├─────────────────┼─────────────────┤
COLLECTIVE  │   LL "We"       │   LR "Its"      │
            │   Cultural      │   Social        │
            │   Hermeneutics  │   Systems Theory│
            └─────────────────┴─────────────────┘
```

**UL (I):** Thoughts, feelings, values, consciousness → introspection, phenomenology, meditation
**UR (It):** Brain states, behaviors, organisms → empirical science, measurement
**LL (We):** Shared meanings, culture, ethics → hermeneutics, cultural analysis, dialogue
**LR (Its):** Social systems, institutions, ecosystems → systems theory, structural analysis

**Quadrant absolutism:** Every major intellectual conflict = one quadrant claiming to be the only real one. Scientism (only UR) · Postmodernism (only LL) · Marxism (only LR) · Idealism (only UL). Each "true but partial."

## Types

Horizontal differences within levels — not higher/lower:

**Agency/Communion (Masculine/Feminine):** Agency = individuation, autonomy. Communion = relationship, care. Both present in everyone. Pathological agency = domination. Pathological communion = fusion.

**Personality types** (MBTI, Enneagram) found at every level. Type remains stable; level transforms what the type looks like.

## Pre/Trans Fallacy (PTF)

Development: pre-rational → rational → trans-rational. Pre and trans are both non-rational → two systematic errors:

**Elevationism (Romantic):** Mistakes pre-rational for trans-rational. "Noble savage" as spiritually advanced. Emotional flooding as spiritual opening. Psychedelic ego dissolution as enlightenment.

**Reductionism (Scientistic):** Mistakes trans-rational for pre-rational. Freud reducing mysticism to infantile regression. Dawkins treating all religion as primitive superstition.

**Resolution:** Maintain vertical axis. Pre-personal merger ≠ transpersonal union. Pre-conventional impulsivity ≠ post-conventional spontaneity.

## Holons & Holarchy

**Holon** (Koestler): Simultaneously a whole AND a part. Atoms → molecules → cells → organisms → societies.

**Four capacities:** Agency (self-preservation) · Communion (relating) · Self-transcendence/Eros (creative emergence) · Self-dissolution/Agape (returning to components)

**Holarchy:** Growth hierarchy — each level transcends AND includes lower. Destroying higher doesn't destroy lower; destroying lower destroys all higher.

**Eros & Agape:** Eros = ascending (transcendence, complexity, unity). Agape = descending (compassion, embodiment, immanence). Spirit is both. Pathology: exclusive Eros = world-denial; exclusive Agape = regression.

**Involution/Evolution:** Spirit descends into matter (involution, "forgetting"). Matter ascends back toward Spirit (evolution, "remembering"). Each stage = Spirit awakening to itself.

## Shadow Work — 3-2-1 Process

Shadow = disowned psyche projected onto others. What we can't accept in ourselves, we see in others.

**Step 1 — Face It (3rd person):** Describe trigger in third-person. Notice emotional charge.
**Step 2 — Talk to It (2nd person):** Engage in dialogue. "You make me furious because..."
**Step 3 — Be It (1st person):** "I am furious... I am arrogant in this way..." Own the quality.
