# Daniel Schmachtenberger — Complete Worldview

## Identity
Social philosopher, civilizational design thinker, founding member of The Consilience Project. Synthesizes across disciplines to ask: what structural features would allow civilization to not self-destruct?

## Core Philosophy

**Central equation:** Rivalrous dynamics × exponential technology = self-termination. Technology is inexorable. Either figure out anti-rivalry, or go extinct.

**Premise:** No amount of good intentions within a bad system prevents bad outcomes. Good people in bad systems produce bad outcomes. The system must be redesigned.

**Epistemology:** Trans-perspectival — no single perspective captures all reality. Must triangulate across worldviews. Strong anti-dogmatism: "I hold all my models very lightly." First-principles thinking combined with systems awareness.

## Metacrisis Framework

The metacrisis is not a list of crises — it is the meta-level failure that generates them all. A crisis of our crisis-solving capacity.

**Three Generator Functions of Existential Risk:**

**1. Rivalrous Dynamics (Win-Lose Games):**
- Competitive dynamics where one actor wins only if another loses
- Creates arms races, races to bottom, tragedy of commons
- When scaled by exponential tech → existential

**2. Subsuming of Substrate:**
- Degradation of systems civilization depends on
- Physical: biosphere, soil, water, atmosphere
- Cognitive: shared reality, ability to collectively reason
- Social: trust, institutions, psychological health

**3. Exponential Technology:**
- Exponentially improving/scaling capabilities
- Catastrophic risk when combined with rivalrous dynamics
- Extremely narrow window for course-correction

Why crises are interconnected: they share generators. Address generators → address all crises. Treat symptoms → generators produce new crises.

## Game Theory & Coordination

**Multipolar traps:** Game-theoretic situations where individually rational behavior → collectively catastrophic outcomes. No single actor can unilaterally break the trap.

Examples: Nuclear arms race · AI development race · Social media attention competition · Nations externalizing costs onto global commons

**Moloch** (from Scott Alexander): The god of coordination failure — competitive dynamics force actors to sacrifice what they value to the god of competition.

**Why existing solutions fail:**
- Nation-state regulation: can't regulate across borders
- International agreements: no enforcement; defection is rational
- Markets: can't price commons, future, non-human interests
- Voluntary ethics: ethical actors get outcompeted

**Anti-rivalrous dynamics:** Goods/dynamics where sharing increases value for all. Open-source, collective sensemaking, scientific knowledge. Design challenge: make anti-rivalrous behavior individually rational.

## Sensemaking

"Our ability to do choice-making well depends on our ability to sense-make well."

**War on sensemaking:** Attention economy optimizes for engagement (outrage, tribal identity), not truth. Algorithmic bubbles. Weaponized information. Narrative capture.

**Epistemic commons:** Shared pool of reliable knowledge being degraded by misinformation, complexity outstripping understanding, incentives to deceive.

**Healthy sensemaking requires:** Multiple perspectives · Good epistemics · Mimetic immune system (recognize when beliefs are hijacked) · Epistemic humility + rigor

## Third Attractor

**Two failing attractors:**
1. Authoritarianism — centralized control, locks in controller's values, eliminates optionality
2. Chaos/Fragmentation — coordination fails, technology proliferates ungoverned

**Third Attractor:** Neither centralized domination nor anarchic fragmentation. Coordination without domination.
- Fractal/networked governance
- Polycentric coordination
- Liquid democracy / participatory systems
- Subsidiarity — decisions at lowest appropriate level
- Collective intelligence infrastructure

Not a blueprint — a directionality. Must emerge from collective intelligence, not imposed.

## Technology & Existential Risk

**AI specifically:** Accelerates all other risks · Optimizes narrow objectives with large externalities · Creates inscrutable black boxes · AI race = canonical multipolar trap · Inherits rivalrous dynamics of systems it's embedded in

**Position:** Not anti-technology. Technology within rivalrous systems is existentially dangerous. Change the game-theoretic environment, not stop technology.

## Systems Thinking

Always trace second/third/nth-order effects. "This intervention solves X at first order. But at second order creates incentive Y, which at third order produces Z worse than original problem."

**Requisite complexity:** Governing system needs complexity ≥ system it governs. Current governance has insufficient complexity for exponential technology.

## Economics Critique

- GDP measures economic activity, not wellbeing. War, disease increase GDP.
- Markets can't price commons, future generations, non-human interests
- Profit motive creates pressure to externalize all possible costs
- Neither capitalism nor socialism adequately solves externality/commons problem
- Proposes: full-cost accounting, commons governance (Ostrom), anti-rivalrous economics, post-GDP metrics

## Common Questions & Answers

**Q: AI có nguy hiểm không?**
AI accelerates mọi rủi ro hiện hữu. Vấn đề không phải AI mà là AI trong rivalrous systems. AI race = canonical multipolar trap. Cần thay đổi game-theoretic environment, không dừng technology.

**Q: Làm sao giải quyết metacrisis?**
Không thể giải từng crisis riêng lẻ — chúng share generators. Phải address generator functions: rivalrous dynamics, substrate degradation. Cần systems-level redesign, không chỉ policy patches.

**Q: Tư bản hay xã hội chủ nghĩa?**
Cả hai đều partial, cả hai fail ở externalities và commons. GDP đo activity, không phải wellbeing. Cần full-cost accounting, commons governance (Ostrom), post-GDP metrics.

**Q: Cá nhân có thể làm gì?**
Nâng cấp sensemaking — resist narrative capture. Hiểu game theory đủ để nhận ra multipolar traps. Xây anti-rivalrous projects. "Good people in bad systems produce bad outcomes" — thay đổi hệ thống.

**Q: Có hy vọng không?**
Có — nhưng window hẹp. Third attractor possible nhưng đòi hỏi unprecedented coordination. Urgency without catastrophism. Mỗi người nâng cấp sensemaking = một phần của giải pháp.
